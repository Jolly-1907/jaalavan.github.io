S&C Electric

As a Lean Process Systems Assistant, I supported manufacturing and process-improvement initiatives through production simulation, time studies, data analysis, workflow documentation, and the development of practical tools for production and engineering teams.

Overview

During my co-op experience at S&C Electric, I worked on lean manufacturing and process-improvement initiatives supporting Metal Enclosed Switchgear production. My primary project involved developing a FlexSim simulation of approximately 20 assembly workstations using current-state production data to evaluate material flow, workstation capacity, production constraints, and possible future-state configurations.

To establish representative inputs for the simulation, I conducted approximately 6–11 time studies per workstation and analyzed cycle time, takt time, throughput, downtime, and process variation. The analysis identified the grinding process as a production bottleneck and provided a data-based foundation for evaluating improvement opportunities.

I modeled and compared future-state production scenarios, developing a configuration that achieved 106% of the required production target. I documented the findings using process maps, workflow documentation, and engineering reports to communicate the proposed production approach and its expected impact.
